About the course

A practical, mentor-led path into product design.

What is this course?

The Graphic Design Program is a 1-month studio-style course that turns beginners into hireable graphic designers. You master Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, Figma, brand systems, typography, layout and print production by shipping 4 real client briefs with weekly mentor critique.

Who can join?

Made for students, freshers, freelancers and creative switchers. You need no art degree, no drawing skills — just curiosity and 8–10 hours a week. Over 65% of our students land freelance work or full-time roles within 2–3 months of finishing.
