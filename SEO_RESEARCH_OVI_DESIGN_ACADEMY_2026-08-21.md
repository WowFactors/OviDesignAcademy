Date: August 21, 2026

Scope:
- Local repo audit of the current static site build
- Fresh Google/market research using current search results on August 21, 2026
- Focus on Chennai rankings for UX/UI, AI design, product design, vibe coding, and no-code terms

Limits:
- I could not use Ahrefs/Semrush/GSC/GBP admin data from this environment, so keyword volumes, backlinks, and indexation breadth are directional rather than tool-verified.
- Google Business Profile strength is inferred from public search visibility and on-site review surfacing, not from GBP backend access.

## Executive Summary

Ovi has a credible positioning advantage, but Google is currently getting mixed signals.

What is working:
- Strong differentiation around AI-powered UX/UI, mentor-led training, student work, and live reviews
- Clear Chennai relevance through NAP, local address, and course intent
- Distinct course pages for the main UX/UI track and advanced track

What is suppressing ranking growth:
- Multiple publicly discoverable entry points compete with the homepage intent: `/`, `/index.html`, `Index-1.html`, `design-courses-in-chennai.html`, and legacy `/new/` URLs
- Messaging and duration claims are inconsistent across live/public versions and local files
- NAP is inconsistent, especially phone/WhatsApp/address details on FAQ vs the rest of the site
- Several supporting pages are not aligned to search demand and are weak for internal keyword reinforcement
- The site is still trying to rank the homepage and course pages for overlapping head terms

The most important near-term move is to consolidate authority into one primary Chennai landing page for `UI UX Design Course in Chennai`, keep the masterclass page as the main course conversion page for the same cluster or shift the homepage to brand/category intent only, and remove legacy indexed variants from competition.

   - demo class dates

- Serving Chennai learners from Kolathur, Anna Nagar, Perambur, Villivakkam, Ambattur, and North Chennai
