About the course

A practical, mentor-led path into product design.

What is this course?

The Web Design Program is a 2-month cohort focused on designing and launching real websites. You master Figma, Framer, Webflow, responsive layout, CMS, animations and SEO basics — shipping 4 live sites with weekly 1:1 mentorship instead of passive tutorials.

Who can join?

Perfect for freelancers, marketers, founders, students and career switchers who want to build beautiful sites fast. No coding, no dev background needed — just 8–10 hours a week. Over 55% of our students book their first paid client within 2–3 months of finishing.
